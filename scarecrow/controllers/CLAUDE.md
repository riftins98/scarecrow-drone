# controllers

Flight control algorithms for GPS-denied indoor navigation. Most controllers are pure computation — they take sensor data in and return VelocityCommand out. Small async executor helpers may live here when they are reusable pursuit behaviors shared by flight scripts.

## Files
- `__init__.py` — Exports: CornerApproachController, DistanceStabilizerController, DistanceTargets, rotate_90, VelocityCommand, WallFollowController, pursuit-entry planner types, and target-pursuit types.
- `corner_approach.py` — Pure lidar controller for safe rear-left/rear-right corner approach. Treats rear/side targets as a safe band, caps combined diagonal speed, and brakes when closing too quickly near the target distances.
- `wall_follow.py` — PD controller maintaining target distance from wall. Outputs body-frame velocity with SVD-based yaw correction. Has `done` property for front-wall stop. Configurable: side (left/right), target_distance, forward_speed, kp/kd gains.
- `target_pursuit.py` — Compatibility wrapper for legacy imports of the target pursuit controller.
- `pursuit/target.py` — Pure vision+lidar target pursuit controller. Consumes latest target observation plus lidar scan and outputs `VelocityCommand` with target alignment, approach, missing-target/search, timeout, and wall-safety state. Target-distance success takes priority over side-wall safety once the front lidar reaches the requested stop distance.
- `pursuit/entry_planner.py` — Pure pursuit-entry planner. Converts target image bearing and calibrated bbox range into approach/advance/reject decisions before the pursuit controller starts.
- `pursuit/entry_executor.py` — Async pursuit-entry helpers for controlled wall-follow advance, slow entry-yaw target reacquire, and fresh target observation handoff.
- `point_navigator.py` — Async `fly_to_point()` and `reverse_wall_follow_to_point()`. A position controller proposes a body-frame velocity and the lidar may veto it per-axis; no scan means no movement at all. Returns a result dict (reached/blocked/timeout + final pose) because missions record it in the map.
- `corner_stabilizer.py` — Async `approach_start_corner()` / `stabilize_corner()`: the loops that drive the pure `corner_approach` and `distance_stabilizer` controllers. Both hold altitude while they work, which the pure controllers cannot do (they emit horizontal velocity only).

  `stabilize_corner()` holds still for `CORNER_SETTLE_S` (3.0s) before correcting position. It runs straight after a 90-degree rotation, which is the worst moment to trust the estimator: rotational flow contaminates optical flow's translational estimate. Measured across a corner, the drone's reported body-frame velocity disagreed with what the lidar saw by up to **0.30 m/s**, decaying to zero by ~t+7s. Correcting during that window means flying to numbers that are wrong and then unwinding it — one corner cost 40s that way. **This is not fixable in the controller**: the lidar was right all along, so no gain tuning or alignment helps. Flight-measured across four corners per run: 0s -> 81.5s, 2.0s -> 58.1s, **3.0s -> 44.5s and 44.9s on repeat**, 4.0s -> 75.3s. Longer is not better — with no position control during the hold the drone drifts, so an over-long settle starts the correction from further away.
- `rotation.py` — Precise 90-degree rotation using compass (coarse turn to ~95 degrees overshoot) + lidar SVD wall alignment (fine-tune). Async function `rotate_90()` takes drone + lidar. Works for both left and right turns. Also `rotate_to_yaw()` (drive to an *absolute* PX4 yaw, for restoring a remembered heading — no lidar, since the target heading may face no visible wall) and `rotate_relative_90()` (adapter so callers holding a `Drone` can reach `rotate_90`, which takes a raw `System`).
- `distance_stabilizer.py` — Multi-constraint hover positioning using lidar distances. Supports any combination of front/rear/left/right targets. Reports `done` when all constraints within tolerance for stable_time seconds.
- `front_wall_detector.py` — Robust front obstacle detection with DBSCAN-style clustering and temporal confirmation (confirm_cycles). Prevents false stops from off-axis obstacles or floor returns.
